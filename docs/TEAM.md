# Team ownership lanes

These are **starting proposals for kickoff discussion**, not fixed assignments. Each teammate should leave BUILD with a substantive piece of the product they can explain and defend.

## John Flack — Team Lead / Architecture & Evaluation
Suggested ownership:
- facilitate scope, owners, decisions and blockers
- maintain the end-to-end architecture
- own evaluation methodology and integration
- help the team preserve human-review boundaries
- make sure every teammate has visible contribution space

## Hans Rony — Product & AI Integration
Suggested ownership:
- reviewer workflow and interaction design
- application logic and model/provider integration
- usability of the claim → evidence → decision path
- help define the Nov. 6 hackathon product slice

## Kristan Mack — Evidence & Delivery
Suggested ownership:
- questionnaire/evidence corpus quality
- evidence manifest and traceability
- product brief, reproducibility and README quality
- delivery package consistency

## Maimuna Chowdhury — Security & Verification
Suggested ownership:
- threat model, permissions and secrets handling
- abuse/misuse cases and security tests
- deployment-security review
- evaluation support and verification checks

## Shared ownership
Everyone:
- manually labels part of the 20-document gold set
- has their own GitHub contribution trail
- reviews at least one teammate PR
- helps build at the Nov. 6 hackathon
- contributes to demo/storytelling
- can explain the full MVP, not just their lane

At kickoff, ask each person what they most want to build. Adjust lanes accordingly.
